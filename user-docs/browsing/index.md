# Browsing the Catalog

The home page is your library. Every game is a card with its cover art; the grid reflows to fit everything from phones to wide desktops. You can move through the grid with the **arrow keys** and open a game with **Enter**.

## Search and genres

Above the grid you find two filters:

- **Search** — type part of a game title (or its disc serial number). Results filter as you type.
- **Genre chips** — a single-select row of genres found in the library. Pick one to narrow the grid, or **All** to see everything.

Combining a search term with a genre works too. If nothing matches you will see _"No games match those filters."_

The library loads continuously as you scroll — keep scrolling and more titles appear.

## Favorites

Favorites are per-account and power the **Favorites row** at the top of the browse page.

To favorite a game:

- **From the grid:** hover a game card and click the **heart** button in its top-right corner.
- **From the game's detail page:** click the **heart** next to the title.

A filled heart means the game is in your favorites; click again to remove it. Favorited games appear in a horizontally scrolling row under "Favorites" — quick access to the games you keep returning to.

::: tip
The heart button and the Favorites row only appear when you are [signed in](/getting-started/).
:::

## The game detail page

Click any cover to open the game's detail page. From top to bottom:

- **Hero image** with the cover art, genre, title, and feature highlights (for example vibration support).
- **Developer, publisher, and release year** line, with the favorite heart next to it.
- **Play buttons** — see [Playing Games](/playing/):
  - **Play** for single-disc games.
  - **Start Disc N** with a disc dropdown for multi-disc games.
  - **Continue** when you have a save state for this game — jumps straight back into your latest save.
- **Description** of the game.
- **Game Information** panel: developer, publisher, first release, players, disc count, and region (NTSC-U, NTSC-J, or PAL).
- **Digital Manual / Strategy Guide** buttons when documents exist for the game. Click to read the PDF right in the page, or use the download button to save it.
- **Screenshots** — click any screenshot to open it full-size and page through the rest.

## When something looks wrong

- A short _"Couldn't reach the server"_ panel with a **Retry** button means the library could not be loaded — check your connection and retry.
- A link that ends on _"Game not found"_ usually means the title was removed from the catalog. Head back to browse.
