Nightstone Studios • Sony Computer Entertainment • 1998

In the shattered world of Obsidian Legacy, you awaken as a forsaken knight bound to the fading embers of a lost empire. Traverse desolate landscapes, uncover forbidden arcana, and face colossal adversaries in a grueling quest to restore the balance of power. The ruins whisper of a time before the cataclysm, where ancient technology and dark magic intertwined.

Experience a masterfully remastered classic, bringing the iconic 1998 action RPG into the modern era with breathtaking visuals, enhanced combat mechanics, and a meticulously restored orchestral soundtrack. Will you rekindle the flame, or let the darkness consume what little remains?
